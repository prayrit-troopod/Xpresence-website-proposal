The Brief, In One Line

Xpresence is entering the D2C perfume market with three fragrances — Power, Focus and Warmth — plus a Discovery Set of 10 ml samples as an easy entry product. As a new brand with no purchase history, the site's job is to communicate what each fragrance stands for, help customers choose confidently, and build enough trust to convert a first-time visitor.

Project objectives
Brand & Trust
•Premium D2C identity from day one
•Trust-building for first-time customers
•Easy fragrance selection, less confusion
•Discovery Set as a low-risk first purchase
Experience & Growth
•Mobile-first shopping experience
•Smoother Add to Cart & checkout
•Scalable Shopify foundation
•Analytics for future CRO decisions
02 · Brand Challenge & Customer Journey

Selling a feeling customers can't smell

Perfume is hard to sell online because customers can't physically smell it. The site does the job a store associate normally would — communicating personality, mood, occasion and trust.

Positioning direction (tone only — not fragrance-note claims)
Power
A stronger, more confident presence.
Focus
A clear, composed, intentional presence.
Warmth
An approachable, welcoming presence.
Actual fragrance notes, ingredients and performance claims will only go live once confirmed by Xpresence — see Section 10.

1

2

03 · Reference Website Analysis

Same premium feel, sharper execution

Xpresence wants a website in the same league as the shared reference site. We studied it closely for design direction, storytelling and feature set — this section is what we'll take inspiration from, and what we'll fix.

What the reference does well
•Premium perfume presentation & emotional storytelling
•Mood-based fragrance discovery
•Clear fragrance-note communication
•Strong Discovery Set positioning
•Solid product education content
•Trust cues — shipping, COD, secure payment
Where it falls short — and what Xpresence will fix
→Cluttered journeys with too many detours before purchase
→No direct side-by-side comparison of its fragrances
→Storytelling and shopping blur into each other
→Repetitive content copy-pasted across product pages
→Weak, inconsistent mobile CTA placement
→Delivery & return info buried, hard to find
→Reviews scattered, not structured as social proof
→Little to no gifting or bundle merchandising
Bottom Line
Match the reference on premium look & storytelling. Fix the gaps above. Use it for direction only — nothing copied.
